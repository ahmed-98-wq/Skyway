Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

Last updated: July 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you contact us through the contact form, WhatsApp, or email, we may collect personal data such as your full name, email address, phone number, and the type of service requested. We do not collect any financial or sensitive data directly through the website.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the submitted data only to respond to your inquiry, provide price quotes, and follow up on the travel, visa, or booking arrangements you request. We do not use your data for any marketing purpose without your explicit consent, and we do not sell or share it with any third party for commercial purposes.

3. Sharing Data With Third Parties

We may share some of your data (such as your name and passport details when needed) with the relevant official bodies (such as embassies, airlines, or hotels) only to the extent necessary to complete the service you requested, and after your consent.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access. However, the security of data transmitted over the internet cannot be guaranteed 100%.

5. Cookies and Analytics Tools

This website uses essential cookies to improve your browsing experience, as well as analytics tools such as Google Analytics via Google Tag Manager, to understand how visitors use the site and to improve our services. These tools may collect non-personal data such as device type, pages visited, and browsing duration. We do not use this data to identify you personally, and you can control or disable cookies from your browser settings at any time.

6. Your Rights

You have the right at any time to request access to the data we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to request its complete deletion, by contacting us directly via email or WhatsApp.

7. Contact Regarding Privacy

For any inquiry related to this privacy policy, you can contact us via email at info@skyway.com or on WhatsApp at +256 794 747 777.
